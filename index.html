From Manual Utility Management to Intelligent Automation: Dominion Financial

01 / Case Study

Meet Dominion Financial

Dominion Financial is a real estate financing company serving investors and real estate professionals, with operations connected to a substantial portfolio of residential properties.

Managing a portfolio of this scale requires more than financing expertise. Behind the scenes, hundreds of properties generate utility bills, payment responsibilities, accounting records, and maintenance-related tasks that must be handled accurately and consistently.

With utility management workflows spanning more than 900 properties, Dominion Financial partnered with Strat AI Solutions to modernize a critical part of its operations through custom automation.

02 / Case Study

The Challenge: Managing Utilities Across 900+ Properties

Imagine managing electricity and water accounts for more than 900 properties, each with different occupancy statuses, payment responsibilities, and billing activity.

For Dominion Financial, utility management involved coordinating information across multiple disconnected systems, including property management software, utility provider portals, and accounting tools.

The process required several important steps:

  • Determining whether Dominion or a tenant was responsible for each utility bill.
  • Retrieving electricity and water bills from separate provider portals.
  • Identifying abnormal consumption that could indicate leaks or maintenance issues.
  • Reviewing and approving payments while avoiding incorrect charges.
  • Recording transactions accurately against individual properties.
  • Preparing tenant notifications and maintenance work orders when necessary.

At this scale, repetitive administrative work can consume significant employee time and create opportunities for errors.

The challenge wasn't simply paying utility bills. It was coordinating an entire operational workflow across hundreds of properties without losing accuracy, visibility, or control.

Dominion needed a solution that could connect its existing systems, handle repetitive tasks automatically, and keep its team involved where human judgment mattered.

03 / Case Study

The Solution: One Custom-Built Automated System

Rather than introducing another disconnected software platform, Strat AI Solutions developed a custom utility management system designed around Dominion Financial's existing operations.

The completed system brings several previously separate processes into one coordinated workflow.

Automated Bill Collection

The system retrieves electricity and water billing information from BGE and Baltimore City utility portals, eliminating the need to individually navigate hundreds of accounts as part of the intended workflow.

Intelligent Payment Routing

By connecting with Dominion's existing property management systems, the automation determines payment responsibility using occupancy information, property records, and billing dates. Tenant-responsible accounts can be excluded from Dominion's payment workflow.

Automatic Exception Detection

The system evaluates utility consumption against established thresholds, flagging unusual activity that may require investigation. Depending on the situation, it can prepare notifications or maintenance work orders for staff review.

Human-Approved Payments

Automation handles bill collection and payment preparation, while Dominion's authorized employees retain control over payment execution. The system generates a consolidated report for review before approved payments are submitted.

Streamlined Accounting

Instead of preparing hundreds of individual accounting entries, the system generates a QuickBooks-compatible journal entry file, allowing property-level expenses to be imported in a single step.

Centralized Reporting and Accountability

A consolidated reporting workflow provides visibility into payment statuses, tenant responsibilities, and exceptions. Activity records are designed to be stored in Dominion's existing database, supporting traceability and accountability.

The result of the development engagement is a completed, custom-built system designed to handle a complex, multi-platform operational process while preserving human oversight.

04 / Case Study

The Results: Built and Ready for Deployment

Strat AI Solutions has completed development of Dominion Financial's custom utility management automation system.

The completed build establishes the foundation for several significant improvements:

900+ Properties, One Coordinated Workflow

The system is designed to manage utility-related processes across more than 900 properties through a unified automation workflow rather than a collection of disconnected tasks.

A Major Reduction in Manual Processing Potential

Automated bill retrieval, payment preparation, responsibility checks, and document generation are designed to reduce the repetitive administrative work associated with utility management.

Simplified Property-Level Accounting

The QuickBooks integration is designed to replace hundreds of individual journal entries with one import per processing cycle.

Greater Visibility Into Problems

Automated exception detection and consolidated reporting are designed to help Dominion's team identify unusual utility consumption, payment issues, and accounts requiring attention.

Automation Without Sacrificing Control

Human approval remains central to the payment process, ensuring that critical financial decisions stay in the hands of authorized employees.

This project demonstrates what's possible when automation is built around a company's actual operations instead of forcing a business to adapt to generic software.

FAQ

Questions before we get started?

/01 What does this cost?

The first hour of audit and scoping is free for qualifying teams. After that, rates are $100–$200 per hour, with hours depending on scope. Delivery typically ranges from $10,000–$50,000, with monthly maintenance typically 5–10% of the delivery cost.

/02 What if it doesn't work?

We agree on the scope and success criteria before building. After delivery, we monitor the solution and address issues through ongoing maintenance, with support terms defined in your project agreement.

/03 What does delivery involve?

We build a custom solution around the scope developed with your operators: implemented on your tools, tested, deployed, and introduced to your team with documentation and training.

/04 What counts as a workflow?

A workflow is a connected sequence of tasks, such as collecting documents, routing follow-ups, or updating deal status. We define the steps and integrations during audit and scoping rather than applying a fixed limit to every project.

/05 Do you replace our tools?

No. We build on your existing stack (Google or Microsoft 365, CRM, Calendly, DocuSign, Make or Zapier, email).

/06 What if we don't have a real stack yet?

We’ll review what you have during the intro call and audit. If foundational tools are needed, we’ll discuss them as part of the scope and implementation plan.

/07 Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Delivery and maintenance includes training, monitoring, and fixes so your team can continue using a working solution. The ongoing scope and monthly cost are agreed with you before the engagement.

/08 How fast can we start?

Start with a 15–20 minute intro call. If we’re a fit, we’ll arrange the first free hour of audit and scoping and confirm the schedule, access requirements, and next steps together.

/09 How do you handle edge cases and failures?

We implement retries, backoff, and manual fallback paths so workflows stay operationally reliable.

/10 What happens after delivery?

We train your team and provide documentation, then stay involved to monitor the solution and fix issues under your maintenance agreement. Monthly maintenance is typically 5–10% of the delivery cost.
